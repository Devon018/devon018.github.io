Research focus

Scalable robot learning for dexterous and contact-rich manipulation, including policy composition, diffusion and vision-language-action policies, robot data engines, and sim-to-real transfer.

Research experience

Carnegie Mellon University, Robotics Institute, Pittsburgh, PA · Jun 2026–Present
Advisors: David Held and Zackory Erickson

  • Developing a force-aware residual controller on top of a frozen diffusion policy for contact-rich insertion.
  • Building simulation, force-processing, recurrent-policy, and evaluation infrastructure in Isaac Gym.

Axis Robotics · Research Intern · Jan 2026–Jun 2026

  • Co-developed AXIS, a community-driven robot data engine integrating browser-based teleoperation, automated task generation and validation, trajectory refinement, and visual and physics-based augmentation.
  • Contributed to dataset construction and vision-language-action policy evaluation across 207 tasks and 50K+ trajectories under a fixed held-out protocol.

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill · Feb 2025–Present
Advisor: Mingyu Ding, Assistant Professor

  • Proposed and implemented DexCompose, a role-aware framework that reuses pretrained dexterous policies through release-test finger attribution, action ownership masks, and dual residual stabilizers.
  • Evaluated 16 composite tasks in Isaac Lab with a Shadow Hand, achieving 77.4% average success and outperforming the strongest baseline by 15.8 percentage points.
  • Led method design, implementation, large-scale simulation experiments, and paper writing; also optimized diffusion guidance to mitigate “ghost-state” drift in contact-rich rollouts.

Shanghai Innovation Institute, Shanghai, China · Feb 2025–Jan 2026
Advisor: Yue Gao, Associate Professor

  • Developed multimodal emotion-recognition and vision-language-action models for situated human-robot interaction.
  • Built a benchmark for evaluating multimodal large language models as interactive agents in situated dialogue.
